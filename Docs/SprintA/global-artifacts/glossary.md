# Glossary

|       **_TEA_**(EN)        |                                                       **_DESCRIPTION_**                                                        |
|:--------------------------:|:------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------:|
|     **Anesthesiology**     |                       Medical specialization focused on pain relief and patient sedation during surgery.                       |
|         **Audit**          |                 A systematic review or assessment of records, often for compliance or performance monitoring.                  |
|   **Availability Slots**   |                            List of time slots the staff defines as being available for appointments                            |
|       **Cardiology**       |                           Medical specialization focused on heart health and cardiovascular system.                            |                                                                          |
| **Critical care medicine** |                    Medical field dealing with critically ill patients, often in intensive care units (ICU).                    |
|      **Dermatology**       |                               Medical specialization focused on skin, hair, and nail disorders.                                |
|   **Emergency medicine**   |                      Specialization in acute care and immediate medical attention for urgent conditions.                       |
|     **Endocrinology**      |                        Medical field that focuses on hormone-related diseases and the endocrine system.                        |
|    **Family medicine**     |                    Primary care specialization providing ongoing health care for individuals and families.                     |
|    **Gastroenterology**    |                           	Medical specialization focused on the digestive system and its disorders.                           |
|          **GDPR**          |                       General Data Protection Regulation: EU law governing data privacy and protection.                        |
|    **General surgery**     |                Surgical specialization dealing with a wide range of conditions requiring operative procedures.                 |
|       **Geriatrics**       |                                    Medical field focused on health care of elderly people.                                     |
|      **Gynaecology**       |                                     Medical field focusing on women's reproductive health.                                     |
|       **Hematology**       |                                     Medical specialization concerned with blood disorders.                                     |
|          **IAM**           |                     Identity and Access Management: system for managing user identities and access rights.                     |
|          **ICU**           |                           Intensive Care Unit: a specialized hospital ward providing critical care.                            |
|       **Immunology**       |                          	 Medical field dealing with the immune system and immune-related disorders.                          |
|   **Internal medicine**    |                 Medical specialization focused on the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of adult diseases.                  |
|   **Maintenance slots**    |                                            Time slots defined for room maintenance.                                            |
|    **Medical genetics**    |                            Field of medicine focused on genetic disorders and hereditary diseases.                             |
|       **Nephrology**       |                                  Medical field specializing in kidney function and diseases.                                   | 
|    **Nuclear medicine**    |                  Medical specialization using radioactive substances for diagnosis and treatment of diseases.                  |
|       **Obstetrics**       |                                  Field focused on pregnancy, childbirth, and postpartum care.                                  |
|        **Oncology**        |                             	 Medical field that focuses on the diagnosis and treatment of cancer.                             |
|     **Ophthalmology**      |                                	 Medical specialization focused on eye health and vision care.                                 |
|      **Orthopedics**       |               Medical field specializing in musculoskeletal system issues, including bones, joints, and muscles.               |
|  **Out-of-band process**   | A process that occurs outside of the normal workflow, often for special or manual actions (e.g., admin registration of users). |
|       **Pathology**        | 	 Medical field that examines the causes and effects of diseases, typically through lab analysis of tissue and bodily fluids.  |
|       **Pediatrics**       |                    Medical specialization focused on the health care of infants, children, and adolescents.                    |
|    **Planning Module**     |                  Software or module responsible for scheduling and resource allocation (e.g., staff, rooms).                   |
|       **Psychiatry**       |                   Medical field that specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of mental health disorders.                    |
|       **Radiology**        |                	Medical field that uses imaging techniques (e.g., X-rays, MRI) to diagnose and treat diseases.                 |
|        **Urology**         |                     Medical specialization focusing on urinary tract systems and male reproductive organs.                     |
